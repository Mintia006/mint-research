# Mint Research

### AI-Powered Japanese Equity Research Platform

Mint Research is an independent financial research platform designed to streamline fundamental analysis of Japanese listed companies.

**Key capabilities**
- Japanese corporate filings and financial data analysis
- Valuation and company comparisons
- Institutional ownership and positioning research
- Earnings revisions and catalyst tracking
- Patent and supply-chain research
- Evidence-linked investment research briefs

**Technology & Development**

An ongoing independent project combining financial research, data engineering, and AI-assisted analysis.

**Project Status:** Active development

*This repository showcases selected research workflows and technical documentation. Private credentials and production infrastructure are excluded.*
