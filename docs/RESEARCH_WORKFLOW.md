# Mint Research — Research Methodology

## Overview

Mint Research is an independently developed, AI-assisted Japanese equity research platform designed to improve the efficiency, depth, and accessibility of fundamental investment research.

The project focuses on combining fragmented financial, corporate, and industry information into structured, evidence-linked research that supports investment decision-making.

Rather than relying solely on financial statements or market prices, Mint Research aims to identify investment-relevant developments across company operations, supply chains, institutional ownership, and emerging industry trends.

## 1. Data Collection

The research framework incorporates multiple categories of information:

- **Financial Disclosures:** Japanese corporate filings, earnings releases, financial statements, and management guidance.
- **Market Data:** Historical prices, valuation metrics, and financial performance.
- **Earnings Revisions:** Changes in earnings expectations and their implications for market sentiment.
- **Institutional Ownership:** Disclosed shareholder positions and changes in institutional holdings.
- **Corporate Developments:** Capital expenditure, production capacity, acquisitions, and strategic partnerships.
- **Patent Research:** Japanese patent publications and technology developments, where reliable company mappings are available.
- **Industry Research:** Supplier relationships, competitive positioning, and developments affecting broader industries.

Sources include Japanese regulatory disclosures, corporate investor relations materials, and other publicly available information.

Data coverage and integration maturity vary across sources.

## 2. Data Processing and Verification

Collected information is organized by company, reporting period, and source.

The research process emphasizes:

- Maintaining traceability to original disclosures.
- Distinguishing reported facts from interpretation.
- Identifying inconsistencies across data sources.
- Tracking changes in financial and operational indicators.
- Flagging incomplete or uncertain information.

A central objective is to reduce the time required to move from raw information to an investment-relevant conclusion without sacrificing source reliability.

## 3. Fundamental Research

The analytical framework evaluates companies through several complementary perspectives.

### Financial Performance

Analysis of revenue growth, profitability, margins, cash generation, and balance sheet strength.

### Valuation

Assessment of valuation multiples, historical comparisons, and scenario-based assumptions where suitable data is available.

### Competitive Positioning

Examination of industry structure, technological capabilities, operational advantages, and business-model sustainability.

### Catalysts

Identification of developments that could materially affect expectations, including earnings revisions, capacity expansions, product launches, and changes in capital allocation.

### Risks

Consideration of execution risk, competitive pressures, valuation uncertainty, and changes in underlying industry demand.

## 4. Research Output

The intended output is a structured investment research brief containing:

1. Company overview and investment context.
2. Key financial and operational developments.
3. Evidence supporting the central investment thesis.
4. Potential catalysts and expected timelines.
5. Valuation considerations.
6. Principal risks and indicators that could invalidate the thesis.
7. References to supporting source materials.

The objective is to make research conclusions transparent, reproducible, and easier to evaluate critically.

## 5. Research Coverage

Mint Research primarily focuses on Japanese listed equities across multiple industries, including:

- Industrials and manufacturing
- Technology and semiconductors
- Electronics and electronic components
- Energy and infrastructure
- Consumer businesses
- Other sectors presenting compelling fundamental research opportunities

Research topics are selected based on investment relevance rather than industry alone.

## 6. Development Status

Mint Research is an active independent development project.

The prototype includes work across financial data, corporate disclosures, valuation, ownership analysis, events, and patent research.

Ongoing development priorities include improving data coverage, processing reliability, application performance, and research workflows.

Not every research capability described in this methodology is fully automated or production-ready.

## Research Philosophy

The guiding principle behind Mint Research is that better investment decisions begin with better information.

AI should improve the speed and accessibility of research while preserving the importance of primary-source verification, independent judgment, and critical analysis.

The goal is not to automate investment decisions, but to provide analysts with a more comprehensive foundation for making them.
